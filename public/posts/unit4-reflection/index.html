Unit 4 Reflection – Communicating Information

Unit 4 gave me an idea that having knowledge about information is important, but knowing how to share it in the most understandable way is more important. So in this unit, I had the opportunity to complete two projects. Both projects involved working with information but in a slightly different way. I learned that there are many ways of presenting information, depending on what type of presentation will be the most effective.

For my first project, I created a chatbot for future applicants of Azim Premji University. First of all, I didn’t have experience in creating chatbots, therefore, I had to find out how to do it by learning from tutorials. At first I thought it would be difficult. Eventually I managed to figure out everything. Since I wanted my chatbot to be more user friendly, I used the button option so that people would not have to deal with language problems, mainly English and also because my chatbot didn’t understand English. I also decided that including the most essential information in my chatbot will be necessary. Overall, I think that my chatbot is informative enough for those who need it.

The next project required creating a dashboard related to air pollution in India. It was my first time completing a similar assignment. Therefore, I needed some time to learn how to complete this task properly. The biggest challenge was to understand when and what types of graphs should be used. There were some moments when it was not obvious which one to use. Still, during the whole process, I realized how crucial it is to visualize information.

In this unit, I also understood that communication is based upon design. Even if information is accurate and relevant, it will not be useful unless other people can understand it. Apart from that, I have become more experienced in finding ways to learn new things independently.
