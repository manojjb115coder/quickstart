Unit 3 Reflection: Learning From Failure

My perspective on failure changed completely after completing Unit 3. I thought that failure is a mistake and it should be avoided. Therefore,I did not see failure as something useful, instead I saw it as a mistake . After the completion of unit 3, I felt that failure can actually help us to learn many things, if we understand it properly.

Then our group worked on a failure case study, which was the ‘Boeing 737 Max’ case. First I got shocked as I did not expect that such a big failure could happen in a well developed aircraft system. From my viewpoint, this example proves that despite of technological development, overconfidence and ignorance of minor problems may result in significant issues as one single sensor did all the mistakes in this ‘Boeing 737 Max’ case. I am sure that the reason why the accident occurred was not only associated with the malfunction but also because of unawareness of pilots.

While learning this unit, by studying the book  “Right Kind of Wrong: The Science of Failing Well by Amy Edmondson”, one of the ideas that I found very interesting was intelligent failure. Personally, in my opinion I feel this is a very useful concept. With its help, it becomes possible to understand that not all failures are bad. If we try something new and fail, we fail but we can learn from it, then that failure is meaningful. However, when the experience gained during the activity is utilized, it means that the mistake becomes meaningful. Unfortunately,in the Boeing case, I feel many failures were not intelligent because they could have been avoided. 

What else I came to realize is the fact that I also do this when it comes to myself and my studying. Whenever I fail at something, it is usual for me not to pay attention or just continue anyway. But now I understand that this is something that I need to do.

Finally, I need to highlight that discussing failures with other participants is helpful. Sometimes, they can notice aspects which cannot be observed by themselves. Now I don’t see failure  as something negative, but as something that can help me to grow. I feel this mindset will help me not only in studies but also in real life situations.
