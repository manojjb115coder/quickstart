## My Learning Experience

Throughout the first unit of the course, I learned about wikis, Markdown, and the creation of digital knowledge. Before this lab, I have already learned about wikis. Although I have learned about it, I still did not fully comprehend how it actually works. I have never tried Markdown before. At first, I thought that recalling the commands for Markdown would be a little confusing. After trying it several times during the lab, I slowly began to comprehend how it actually works. I learned that Markdown can be used to organize information in a very clear manner.

Another learning experience I got from this course is that I learned about the wiki practice activity and the materials about information and knowledge. From this activity, I learned that information can be organized in a meaningful manner. I learned that I can actually work with different individuals to come up with a meaningful discussion.

I learned about creating a reflection blog with Hugo and GitHub. Although I have never tried creating a blog or a website before, I learned that it can actually be done. At first, I thought that the commands to be followed during the creation of the blog would be very difficult. After following the instructions step by step, I slowly began to comprehend how to create a blog.

I learned about different concepts during the entire process. I learned that learning actually starts with data, then to information, and lastly to knowledge. Throughout the entire process of the lab, I learned that the commands I followed at first were actually information. After I slowly began to comprehend the commands, I learned that they actually turned to knowledge.
