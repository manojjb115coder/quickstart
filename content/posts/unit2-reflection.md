## Unit 2 Reflection – The Nature of Information Problems

In Unit 2,  learning became more practical and easy as compared to Unit 1. Unit 2 is typically learning how information is actually processed and used. This unit involved more technical and practical concepts. From this unit, I realised that working with information involves many challenges such as understanding the problem clearly, choosing the right representation, and mainly interpreting the results correctly.

One important learning for me from this unit was how information can be represented through different forms, especially through images. While learning about image transformation, Before this unit i thought that images are simply pictures but after this unit I learned that image is a collection of pixels values. Each pixel stores numerical information or value, Through this I came to know that visual information depends on numbers. I also learned that changing brightness, contrast, and  applying filters involves mathematical calculation. This made me realize that solving information related problems requires knowledge of mathematical operations and understanding how data is structured and processed.
Another important aspect was the role of number systems like binary, decimal, and hexadecimal. For solving image transformation problems, number conversion is more important. I found this easier because I already learnt these conversions in my last course. And after practicing these became easier for me.

We also worked on **Optimization** problems, where the aim is to find the best solutions among many possibilities. And in that we learned about the Greedy approach (I used this approach many times before learning optimization) .  I found this part easier because the problems were logical and step based. And from this I understood that in real life problems also there may not always be a single correct answer. This showed me that information problems often involve decision making.

Later, I learned about **Modeling and Abstraction**, which helped me to understand how complex problems can be simplified. Modeling means creating a simplified version of a real world system, while abstraction means focusing only on important details and ignores unnecessary one. I found this idea very useful because it showed me that trying to include every detail can make a problem harder to solve. Instead, simplifying the problem can make it easier to understand and analyze.

Overall, Unit 2 helped me understand that information problems are not just about data but about how we represent, process, and interpret that data. It also showed me that solving such problems requires both technical skills and logical thinking. This unit also improved my confidence in handling concepts that involve numbers and problem solving.
